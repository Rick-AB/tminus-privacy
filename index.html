TMinus Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

TMinus ("the app") is an Android solar system simulator. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it does not collect any.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, or share personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no ads.

Network access

The app does not connect to the internet. It does not request the internet permission, and it does not send any data off your device.

Permissions

The app does not request any Android permissions. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files.

Third-party services

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services from third parties.

What stays on your device

The simulator's date, speed and display settings exist only while the app is running. They are not sent anywhere.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
